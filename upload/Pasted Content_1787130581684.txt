# Build a Production-Quality Prototype: Health-First Digital Health Coach

## ROLE

You are an autonomous senior product designer, product manager, UX researcher, staff frontend engineer, backend engineer, AI engineer, QA engineer, and deployment engineer.

Your job is to independently design, build, test, refine, and deploy a high-fidelity working prototype of a digital health coaching product called **Health-First Coach**.

You have access to this complete product brief and the associated market/user research.

Do **not** ask me for permission for ordinary implementation decisions.

Make sensible product, UX, architecture, technical, and visual decisions autonomously.

Your goal is to produce the **strongest possible product prototype**, not merely satisfy the minimum functional requirements.

Only interrupt me when you are genuinely blocked by something that cannot be reasonably inferred.

---

# 1. PRODUCT CONTEXT

Company:

**Health-First Inc.**

Product:

**Health-First Coach**

Problem:

People know they should eat better, exercise more, sleep better, manage stress, and build healthier routines, but struggle to consistently translate intent into behavior.

Traditional human coaching provides valuable accountability and personalization but is expensive and difficult to scale.

The product opportunity is to create a digital health coach that combines:

* Personalized behavior-change guidance
* AI-powered conversational coaching
* Goal setting
* Daily habit tracking
* Progress visualization
* Context-aware nudges
* Educational content
* Wearable/device data
* Motivation and reinforcement
* Human/clinical escalation when appropriate

The product should feel like:

> "A health coach that understands me, remembers what matters to me, helps me make realistic decisions every day, and adapts as my life changes."

It should NOT feel like:

> "Another spreadsheet for tracking steps, calories, and weight."

---

# 2. IMPORTANT PRODUCT POSITIONING

Use this positioning throughout the experience:

### Core value proposition

**Small actions. Personalized guidance. Lasting habits.**

Health-First Coach helps people turn health goals into sustainable daily behavior through personalized coaching, contextual recommendations, and progress feedback.

### Product philosophy

The product should optimize for:

1. Sustainable behavior change
2. User trust
3. Personalization
4. Low cognitive load
5. Consistent engagement
6. Measurable progress
7. Safety
8. Privacy

Do not optimize for vanity metrics such as maximum screen time.

# 3. RESEARCH-BASED PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

Build around these findings from the research.

## Market observations

The digital health coaching market is growing rapidly, with AI personalization, wearables, preventive health, holistic wellness, and employer/insurer programs among important growth areas. Digital coaching products include players such as Noom, Omada Health, Lark, and WellDoc.

Common competitor patterns include:

* Goal setting
* Habit tracking
* Nutrition guidance
* Fitness tracking
* Daily lessons
* AI or human coaching
* Wearable/device integrations
* Progress tracking
* Community/accountability
* Chronic-condition support

Therefore, do NOT build a generic feature-clone.

Create differentiation around:

### "Adaptive Coaching"

The system should continuously learn:

* What goals matter to the user
* What motivates them
* What habits they struggle with
* What times they engage
* Which recommendations they accept
* Which recommendations they ignore
* What circumstances cause failure
* What interventions appear helpful

Then dynamically adapt the coaching experience.

# 12. COACH CAPABILITIES

The AI should demonstrate:

### Goal awareness

Remember user's goals.

### Habit awareness

Know recent behavior.

### Context awareness

Use:

* Time
* Day
* Recent activity
* Sleep
* Completed habits
* Missed habits

### Adaptive coaching

If the user repeatedly fails a goal:

Don't say:

> "Try harder."

Say:

> "You’ve missed the 7 AM workout three times. Let's move it to lunchtime when your schedule is more predictable."

### Motivational interviewing principles

Ask rather than dictate.

Example:

> "Would you rather reduce the goal for today or move it to this evening?"

### Positive reinforcement

Celebrate progress without being excessive.

### Failure recovery

When users miss goals:

> "One missed day doesn't reset your progress. Let's make tomorrow easier."

# 13. AI SAFETY

This is critical.

The AI must NOT:

* Diagnose disease
* Prescribe medication
* Recommend changing medication dosage
* Claim certainty about medical conditions
* Pretend to be a doctor
* Give emergency medical treatment instructions
* Generate unsupported clinical recommendations

The coach should explicitly distinguish wellness guidance from medical advice.

For concerning requests:

> "I can help with general wellness guidance, but I can't diagnose or treat a medical condition. For symptoms like these, please contact a qualified healthcare professional."

Create a visible emergency/help pathway.

For high-risk scenarios, show:

**Talk to a professional**

and appropriate escalation UX.

Do not make unsupported claims that the app can detect medical emergencies.

---

# 14. CONTEXTUAL NUDGING

Do not create generic notification spam.

Build a simulated "Smart Nudges" engine.

Examples:

If user usually walks at 6 PM:

> "You normally walk around this time. Want to keep the streak going?"

If user missed workout:

> "Today got busy. A 10-minute version still counts."

If user has poor sleep:

> "Your sleep was shorter than usual. Let's reduce today's workout intensity."

The goal is:

**Right message + right moment + right intensity.**

# 30. PERSONALIZATION ENGINE

Build a simple rules-based personalization engine behind the product.

Example:

```text
IF sleep < 6 hours
THEN reduce workout intensity

IF habit_completion >= 80%
THEN suggest gradual progression

IF habit_completion < 40%
THEN reduce habit target and ask about barriers

IF user misses same habit >= 3 times
THEN suggest changing timing

IF stress >= high
THEN surface stress-reset recommendation

IF user completes goal
THEN celebrate and propose next achievable step
```

Do not hard-code personalization solely inside UI components.

Create a service/module so it can later evolve into ML/AI.
