    21→# 1. PRODUCT CONTEXT
    22→
    23→Company:
    24→
    25→**Health-First Inc.**
    26→
    27→Product:
    28→
    29→**Health-First Coach**
    30→
    31→Problem:
    32→
    33→People know they should eat better, exercise more, sleep better, manage stress, and build healthier routines, but struggle to consistently translate intent into behavior.
    34→
    35→Traditional human coaching provides valuable accountability and personalization but is expensive and difficult to scale.
    36→
    37→The product opportunity is to create a digital health coach that combines:
    38→
    39→* Personalized behavior-change guidance
    40→* AI-powered conversational coaching
    41→* Goal setting
    42→* Daily habit tracking
    43→* Progress visualization
    44→* Context-aware nudges
    45→* Educational content
    46→* Wearable/device data
    47→* Motivation and reinforcement
    48→* Human/clinical escalation when appropriate
    49→
    50→The product should feel like:
    51→
    52→> "A health coach that understands me, remembers what matters to me, helps me make realistic decisions every day, and adapts as my life changes."
    53→
    54→It should NOT feel like:
    55→
    56→> "Another spreadsheet for tracking steps, calories, and weight."

    98→* Habit tracking
    99→* Nutrition guidance
   100→* Fitness tracking
   101→* Daily lessons
   102→* AI or human coaching
   103→* Wearable/device integrations
   104→* Progress tracking
   105→* Community/accountability
   106→* Chronic-condition support
   107→

   111→
   112→### "Adaptive Coaching"

   115→
   116→* What goals matter to the user

   118→* What habits they struggle with
   119→* What times they engage

   617→* Recent activity
   618→* Sleep
   619→* Completed habits

   621→
   622→### Adaptive coaching
   623→
   624→If the user repeatedly fails a goal:
   625→
   626→Don't say:
   627→
   628→> "Try harder."
   629→

   634→### Motivational interviewing principles
   635→
   636→Ask rather than dictate.
   637→
   638→Example:
   639→
   640→> "Would you rather reduce the goal for today or move it to this evening?"

   642→### Positive reinforcement
   643→
   644→Celebrate progress without being excessive.
   645→
   646→### Failure recovery
   647→
   648→When users miss goals:
   649→
   650→> "One missed day doesn't reset your progress. Let's make tomorrow easier."

   654→# 13. AI SAFETY
   655→
   656→This is critical.
   657→
   658→The AI must NOT:
   659→
   660→* Diagnose disease
   661→* Prescribe medication
   662→* Recommend changing medication dosage
   663→* Claim certainty about medical conditions

   665→* Give emergency medical treatment instructions
   666→* Generate unsupported clinical recommendations

   669→

   671→
   672→> "I can help with general wellness guidance, but I can't diagnose or treat a medical condition. For symptoms like these, please contact a qualified healthcare professional."

   676→For high-risk scenarios, show:
   677→
   678→**Talk to a professional**

   680→and appropriate escalation UX.
