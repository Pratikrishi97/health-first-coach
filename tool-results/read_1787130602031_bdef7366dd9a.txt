   654→   654→# 13. AI SAFETY
   655→   655→
   656→   656→This is critical.
   657→   657→
   658→   658→The AI must NOT:
   659→   659→
   660→   660→* Diagnose disease
   661→   661→* Prescribe medication
   662→   662→* Recommend changing medication dosage
   663→   663→* Claim certainty about medical conditions
   664→   664→* Pretend to be a doctor
   665→   665→* Give emergency medical treatment instructions
   666→   666→* Generate unsupported clinical recommendations
   667→   667→
   668→   668→The coach should explicitly distinguish wellness guidance from medical advice.
   669→   669→
   670→   670→For concerning requests:
   671→   671→
   672→   672→> "I can help with general wellness guidance, but I can't diagnose or treat a medical condition. For symptoms like these, please contact a qualified healthcare professional."
   673→   673→
   674→   674→Create a visible emergency/help pathway.
   675→   675→
   676→   676→For high-risk scenarios, show:
   677→   677→
   678→   678→**Talk to a professional**
